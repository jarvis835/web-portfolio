# Website business playbook (₹0 start)

## What you sell
Simple websites for local businesses that have only an Instagram page or a Google Maps listing, or no online presence at all.

| Package | Price | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹4,999 | 1 page, WhatsApp/call buttons, map, mobile-friendly |
| Business | ₹9,999 | Up to 5 pages, menu/services, enquiry form, Google Business Profile setup |
| Care plan | ₹999/month | Updates, offers, photos, hosting looked after |

Terms: 50% upfront, 50% at launch. Up to 2 rounds of changes. The client buys the domain in their own name (about ₹800 to ₹1,200 a year). Hosting is free on GitHub Pages or Netlify.

**Start cheaper for your first 2 or 3 clients** (₹2,500 to ₹3,500) in exchange for a testimonial and permission to show their site. Raise prices after that.

## Realistic numbers
- Expect about 1 yes for every 20 to 30 businesses you genuinely talk to.
- 3 sites a month at ₹7,000 average is about ₹21,000 a month, plus care plans that build up over time.
- The first client usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Don't quit in week 2.

## Who to approach (in priority order)
1. Businesses you or your family already visit. Warm contacts close fastest.
2. Places on Google Maps with good reviews but **no website link**.
3. Busy Instagram pages for local businesses with no website in the bio.

Good niches: cafes and bakeries, dental and skin clinics, salons, gyms, coaching and tuition centres, interior designers, caterers.

## The method: free mock-up first
Don't pitch "I make websites". Instead, make a 1-page mock-up of *their* business (I can build each one in minutes), then show it to them. Seeing their own name on a real page is what sells it.

## Outreach scripts (send one at a time, personally, no bulk messages)

**WhatsApp / Instagram DM**
> Hi! I came across [Business] on Google Maps. Your reviews are great, but I noticed you don't have a website, so people searching online can't see your menu or timings. I made a quick sample homepage for you, no charge: [link]. If you like it, I can make it live in a week. Happy to change anything. – [Your name]

**In person (walk-in)**
> Hi, I'm [name], I make websites for local shops in [area]. I made a sample one for [Business], can I show you on my phone? It takes 30 seconds.

**Follow-up (after 3 days, only once)**
> Hi, just checking if you had a chance to look at the sample site for [Business]. No pressure at all. Happy to answer any questions.

If they say no, thank them and move on. Never message the same person more than twice.

## Your daily routine (1 to 2 hours)
- Pick 5 businesses. Send me their name, area and Instagram/Maps link.
- I make 5 mock-ups. You send 5 personal messages or visit them.
- Track everything in a simple sheet: name, date contacted, reply, next step.

## Getting paid
UPI is fine to start. Send a simple invoice (I can make one). Once you're earning regularly, read up on registering as a freelancer and filing income tax. Income above the basic exemption limit is taxable.

## Adding reels later
Once you have 2 or 3 clients, offer them 8 reels a month for ₹6,000 to ₹10,000 (scripts, captions, editing). Same customers, bigger monthly income.
